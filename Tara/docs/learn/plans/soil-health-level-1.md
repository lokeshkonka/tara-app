# Level 1 — Meet Your Soil
## Step 1 — What Is Soil?

### Overview
This step introduces the learner to the fundamental concept of soil as a living ecosystem through a progressive Tara Concept Card flow followed by a quick assessment MCQ.

---

### Phase 1: Tara Overview Concept Card
Tara appears with the animated character avatar and paragraph speech card.

**Tara Speech & Text:**
> “Soil is much more than the dirt we see on the ground. It is a living environment where plant roots grow and where air, water, nutrients, organic matter, and tiny living organisms work together. Healthy soil gives plants a place to grow and helps them get the things they need.”

**Progressive Highlight Flow:**
The overview card progressively highlights the key components of soil:
```text
SOIL
 ├── Air
 ├── Water
 ├── Nutrients
 ├── Organic Matter
 └── Living Organisms
```

**Transition Quote:**
> “Now let's take a closer look at what makes up healthy soil.”

---

### Phase 2: Concept Cards Breakdown

#### 1. Air
* **Icon:** `air` (💨)
* **Tara Text:** “Soil has tiny spaces between its particles. These spaces can hold air, which roots and soil organisms need to live.”

#### 2. Water
* **Icon:** `water-drop` (💧)
* **Tara Text:** “Soil stores water and makes it available to plant roots. How much water soil can hold depends on the soil and its condition.”

#### 3. Nutrients
* **Icon:** `eco` (🌿)
* **Tara Text:** “Plants need nutrients to grow. Healthy soil helps store and cycle nutrients so plants can access them.”

#### 4. Organic Matter
* **Icon:** `compost` (🍂)
* **Tara Text:** “Leaves, roots, crop residues, and other natural materials can become organic matter in the soil. It is an important part of a healthy soil system.”

#### 5. Living Organisms
* **Icon:** `bug-report` (🦠)
* **Tara Text:** “Soil is home to many living organisms. They help break down organic materials and play important roles in the soil ecosystem.”

---

### Phase 3: Quick MCQ

**Question:** What can be found in healthy soil?

* A. Only dirt
* B. Only water
* C. Air, water, nutrients, organic matter and living organisms **(Correct)**
* D. Only plant roots

**Evaluation:**
* **Correct Answer:** C
* **Reward:** +20 XP
